ROS domain

    

      
      
    

    
Restarts the server on that domain (robot and running script are stopped).
      The robot's own domain is set on its settings page (http://10.0.109.58/configuration),
      the lidar driver's with DOMAIN=… ./start.sh.

      Drive slowly (≤ 0.15 m/s), turn gently, avoid strafing, and come back to known places.
